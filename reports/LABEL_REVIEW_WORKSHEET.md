# Label review worksheet

This is a review aid, not a source of accepted labels. No decisions have been filled in.

Review measurements and the paper without looking at model alarms. Record your own interval boundaries and evidence first, then compare with the assistant proposals below. A visible change alone does not prove a fault, and a stable trace does not prove normality. Leave uncertain times unknown. The plots summarize 20 ms bins; inspect raw samples for short events or unclear transitions.

For each recording, record two independent assessments and reconcile disagreements. These are waveform-based labels, not exact physical injection timestamps. The two-reviewer convention is our project quality procedure, not a stated course requirement.

## Decisions to record

For each proposed or new interval, provide: start/end seconds; normal, fault or unknown; reviewer names; evidence with waveform/channel details and source page; and whether the decision agrees with, revises or rejects the proposal. Split intermittent events into separate intervals. Never fill unknown gaps automatically.

## Applying completed reviews

Keep this generated worksheet as a template; save completed reviewer notes separately because regeneration replaces this file. Only after evidence-backed review, transfer agreed decisions into annotations/intervals.csv, using reviewed status and distinct reviewer identities. Preserve evidence and keep unresolved intervals unknown. Rerun interval_labels.py, extract_features.py, then baselines.py. Do not approve regions simply to make training possible.

Independent session verification, startup settling and calibration limitations remain separate from this annotation check. Reviewing only visibly obvious faults introduces selection bias. Retain the frozen split and do not inspect test alarm performance to tune choices.
